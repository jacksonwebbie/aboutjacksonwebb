Privacy Statement

How information on this site is handled.

What This Site Collects

This is a personal website. It does not use accounts, cookies for tracking, or advertising. The only information collected is what you choose to enter in the contact form.

Contact Form

The basic information form on the contact page does not store anything on this website. When you press Send, it opens your own email app with your answers filled in, and nothing is sent until you send that email yourself. Messages are only used to reply to you and are never sold or shared.

Google Form

The contact page also includes a Google Form. Responses submitted there are stored in Google Forms, are only used to reply to you, and are never sold or shared. Google's own privacy policy applies to information submitted through it.

Questions

If you have questions about this statement, reach out through the contact page.
